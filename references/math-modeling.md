# Mathematical modeling profile

Use this profile for modeling competitions, operations research, simulation, forecasting, or data-driven modeling projects.

Capture only fields supported by evidence:

- problem and current subproblem;
- data inspected, cleaned, aligned, or derived;
- units and time or spatial granularity;
- modeling assumptions and why they were adopted or rejected;
- decision variables, objectives, constraints, or prediction targets;
- baseline model and candidate improvements;
- solver, algorithm, or computational technique actually used;
- validation, sensitivity, robustness, residual, or backtest evidence;
- figures, tables, paper sections, and submission artifacts advanced;
- modeling risk, ambiguity, data leakage risk, and unresolved limitations;
- contribution to team progress.

Capability statements may include problem decomposition, data reasoning, mathematical formulation, algorithm implementation, experimental design, critical evaluation, technical writing, and collaboration only when the evidence demonstrates them.

Do not describe a proposed model as implemented. Distinguish:

- considered;
- selected;
- formulated;
- coded;
- executed;
- validated;
- included in the paper.

For competition material, treat problem statements, attachments, code, results, and papers as private unless the user separately authorizes publication and redistribution is permitted.
