# Research and coursework profile

Capture the useful subset of:

- research question or learning objective;
- materials, sources, or datasets reviewed;
- method, theorem, framework, experiment, or analytical technique used;
- evidence evaluated and competing interpretations;
- calculation, experiment, assignment, or draft completed;
- feedback received and revision made;
- conceptual difficulty and how it was resolved;
- current conclusion and its limitations;
- next reading, experiment, or deliverable.

Distinguish reading about a method from applying it. Cite sources when the report is intended for formal submission. Do not present unverified notes as established conclusions.
